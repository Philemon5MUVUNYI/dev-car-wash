How your car gets washed

Four stages, always in this order, so grit never gets dragged across your paint.

  1. Pre-soak

    A low-pressure rinse lifts loose mud and sand before anyone touches the paint.

  2. Foam

    Thick pH-neutral foam sits for two minutes and loosens the grime that is stuck on.

  3. Hand wash

    Two buckets and a clean mitt for every panel. One bucket soap, one bucket rinse.

  4. Rinse and dry

    Soft-water rinse, then a microfibre dry. No spots, no streaks.
